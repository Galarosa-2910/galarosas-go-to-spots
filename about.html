About the Guide

John Marco Galarosa

"I don't go outside much, but when I do, I make every second count."

As someone who spends most of my time focused on studies and personal projects, stepping outside isn't an everyday routine. Because of that, whenever I travel or visit a new location, it has to be genuinely worthwhile. The Rare Escapes is a curated collection of places that have left a lasting impression on me, whether as backdrops for creative projects, dependable travel hubs, or familiar urban comfort spots.

Every destination featured here comes from firsthand, grounded experience rather than generic tourist checklists. You can trust these recommendations because they prioritize genuine utility, cultural depth, and practical comfort for students, creators, and everyday commuters.
