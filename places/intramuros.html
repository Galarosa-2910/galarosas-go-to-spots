Intramuros

Why I Recommend It

Having visited Intramuros multiple times while working on creative and film projects, this historic district remains one of the most rewarding escapes in the middle of Manila. Known as the "Walled City," its preserved Spanish colonial architecture, cobblestone paths, and towering stone ramparts offer a rare, peaceful atmosphere that contrasts sharply with the rush of modern city life.

Whether you are looking for creative inspiration, scouting atmospheric backdrops, or simply enjoying a quiet, reflective stroll along centuries-old walls, Intramuros provides plenty of shaded courtyards, heritage cafes, and scenic sunset views along the perimeter.

Visitor Details

Category Information
Location Bonifacio Dr & Padre Burgos Ave, Manila, Metro Manila
Opening Hours Open daily, 6:00 AM – 9:00 PM (Fort Santiago closes at 8:00 PM)
Entrance Fee Free (General Area) / ₱75.00 for Fort Santiago (₱50.00 for Students)
Best Time to Visit Late afternoon (4:00 PM – 6:30 PM) for cooler weather and sunset views
Key Highlights Fort Santiago, San Agustin Church, Baluarte de San Diego, and Plaza Roma
